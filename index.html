Executive summary

The case in four points

Large and fragmented

A fast-growing market with no POS vendor leading every outlet type.

Casual dining is the opening

The largest segment is split across many systems. Hotels and premium dining are locked in.

2027 is a switching year

Margins are under pressure and e-invoicing becomes mandatory — every operator reviews its stack.

Recommendation

Enter in January 2027 through my network, hire 3 sellers in April, and lead with customer data where the POS switch is resisted. Worst case: ~AED 1.12M opex, ~AED 0.75M cash collected, ~AED 0.72M exit ARR.

04 · SWOT*

Strong product, no local proof yet

Strengths

  • Proven at enterprise scale in India
  • Full suite: POS, AI host, guest memory, live P&L
  • Every module works on top of any POS

Weaknesses

  • No UAE references or brand awareness
  • Localisation still to prove: Arabic, e-invoicing, local apps
  • No hotel PMS integration or local support yet

Opportunities

  • Fragmented casual-dining and multi-outlet groups
  • 2027 e-invoicing forces a stack review
  • Restaurants pay per branch, per platform for integrators

Threats

  • Resistance to another POS migration
  • Foodics moving up-market with deep funding
  • Oracle lock-in through PMS links and group contracts

*SWOT Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats
